Your sounds

Import your own bells or ambient loops (mp3, wav, m4a…). Stored privately on this device. On iPhone, pick files from the Files app or your recordings.

Add guided voices

Guided sessions live in your GitHub repo — that's Pranava's backend. Record a voice track (e.g. Voice Memos), upload it to the guided/ folder at github.com/anupbkc/pranava, add one entry in guided.json, and it appears here for every device. Each session can offer several voices (male, female, your own).

Practice report

Account

You're using Pranava without an account — sessions, sounds and your progress report stay private on this device, and everything works offline. Cloud accounts (sign in to sync history across devices) are on the roadmap and will always be optional.

Install on your phone

iPhone: open in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
Android: Chrome menu → Install app.
Pranava then works fully offline, like a native app.
