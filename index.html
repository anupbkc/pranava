Add guided voices

Guided sessions live in your GitHub repo — that's Pranava's backend. Record a voice track (e.g. Voice Memos), upload it to the guided/ folder at github.com/anupbkc/pranava, add one entry in guided.json, and it appears here for every device. Each session can offer several voices (male, female, your own).
