Privacy Policy — Pranava

Last updated: 2026. Pranava is a product of GIRI — Gayatri Interdisciplinary Research Institute.

Summary: Pranava does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. Everything you do stays on your own device. There are no accounts, no servers receiving your data, no analytics, and no advertising trackers.

What we store, and where

Pranava stores the following only on your device, using your browser's local storage and on-device database:

  • Your settings (durations, chosen sounds, voice and volume preferences).
  • Your session history and practice statistics (used to show your own progress report).
  • Any custom breathing patterns you create.
  • Any sound files you import (kept privately on your device; never uploaded anywhere).

This information never leaves your device and is not accessible to us or anyone else. Clearing your browser data, or uninstalling the app, deletes it.

What we do NOT do

  • We do not collect your name, email, location, or any identifying information.
  • We do not use analytics, cookies for tracking, or advertising.
  • We do not transmit your data to any server.

Network use

The app downloads its own files (code, icons, and the built-in guided sessions and sound library) from its hosting so it can work offline afterward. These are ordinary file requests and carry none of your personal data. Optional device features you choose to use — the screen wake lock (to keep the timer awake) and speech synthesis (for the voice guide) — run entirely on your device.

Children

Pranava is a general-audience wellness tool and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If future versions add optional features that involve data (for example an optional account to sync your history across devices), this policy will be updated and such features will be clearly optional and opt-in.

Not medical advice

Pranava is a meditation and breathwork timer for general wellbeing. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, or cure any condition. Consult a qualified professional for medical concerns.
