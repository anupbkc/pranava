# Pranava — Guided Recording Kit

Everything is pre-wired. To make a voice go live, just **record → name the file exactly as below →
drop it in this `guided/` folder** (GitHub website → Add file → Upload files → Commit). It appears in
the app on the next online open. No code, no version bump.

## Voice picker slots already set up (in `guided.json`)
Each session offers **Anup** and **Friend** in the voice dropdown. Until a file exists, tapping that
voice shows "coming soon 🎙️" — nothing breaks.

> Rename "Friend" to her real name anytime: open `guided.json`, change the label `"Friend"` (keep the
> filename the same, or rename both). It's just the text shown in the picker.

## Recording tips
Quiet room · airplane mode · phone ~20 cm away · speak slowly · leave real silences (that space is the
practice) · re-record a line rather than editing · both voices read the *same script* so they're
interchangeable. `(… pause …)` = hold a few silent seconds; `(long pause)` = ~10–30s of silence.

## Script — 5-Minute Grounding
Welcome. (… pause …) Gently, close your eyes. (… pause …) Take three deep breaths with me.
Breathe in, through the nose. (… pause …) And breathe out, through the nose. (… pause …)
Again. Breathe in. (… pause …) And slowly, breathe out. (… pause …)
One more time. Deep breath in. (… pause …) And release. (… pause …)
Now, relax. Feel your body, from your head, to your toes. (… pause …)
The top of your head. Your forehead. Your eyes. Your jaw. (… pause …)
Your shoulders. Your arms. Your chest. Your belly. Your legs. (… pause …)
All the way down, to your toes. (… pause …)
The body, is relaxed. (… pause …) The mind, is calm. (… pause …) The spirit, is positive. (… pause …)
Feel this positive vibe, all around you. Sit with it. (long pause)
Now, gently bring your attention, to your breath — the breath that is naturally flowing.
In. (… pause …) And out. (… pause …) Simply watch the breath. (long pause)
The body is relaxed. The mind is calm. (long pause)
Slowly, bring your awareness back. Feel your fingers, your toes.
And when you are ready, gently open your eyes. Namaste.

## Script — Before Sleep
Welcome. This meditation will guide you gently, towards sleep. (… pause …)
Lie down comfortably, and let your eyes close. Take a slow, deep breath in, through the nose. (… pause …)
And let it out, with a long, soft sigh. (… pause …) Again, breathe in. And sigh it all away. (… pause …)
Feel the weight of your body, sinking into the bed. The day is now complete.
There is nothing more to do. Nothing to solve. Tomorrow can wait. This moment, is only for rest. (… pause …)
A soft, warm wave of relaxation begins at the top of your head. It melts your forehead.
Your eyes grow heavy. Your jaw unclenches. Your shoulders sink. (… pause …)
Your arms grow heavy. Your chest rises and falls, all by itself. Your belly is soft.
Your legs are heavy, like warm sand. Your feet, warm and still. (… pause …)
Now we count breaths, from ten down to one. With each number, drift a little deeper.
Ten. (… pause …) Nine. (… pause …) Eight, softer now. (… pause …) Seven. (… pause …) Six, heavier. (… pause …)
Five. (… pause …) Four, drifting. (… pause …) Three. (… pause …) Two. (… pause …) And one. (long pause)
Let the counting go. Let thoughts drift by, like clouds in a night sky. There is nowhere to go.
Nothing to do. Only rest. (long pause) Resting. (long pause) Goodnight. Sleep well.

## Script — Kundalini Rising
Welcome to this gentle kundalini meditation. Sit with your spine tall, and relaxed.
Gently close your eyes. Take a slow deep breath in, through the nose. (… pause …)
And breathe out, through the nose. Again, breathe in deeply. And let everything go. (… pause …)
Bring your attention to the base of your spine. Imagine there, a small golden light, resting quietly.
This is your dormant energy, your inner power. With every breath in, the light glows brighter. (… pause …)
As you breathe in slowly, feel the golden light begin to rise. Gently. Slowly. Up your spine. (… pause …)
It rises to the belly — warm and calm. (… pause …) It rises to your heart; feel your chest fill with warm
golden light. (… pause …) It rises to your throat, soft and open. (… pause …) It rises to the space between
your eyebrows — still, clear, awake. (… pause …) And now it reaches the crown of your head. Feel the light
blossom above you, like a lotus of a thousand petals. (long pause)
You are a column of golden light. Rooted in the earth, open to the sky.
The body is relaxed. The mind is calm. The spirit is positive. Rest here, in this flow of energy. (long pause)
Simply breathe, and let the light flow up and down your spine. (long pause)
Now slowly let the light settle back down, to the base of the spine. Calm. Contained. Glowing softly.
This power is always within you. Slowly bring your awareness back. Feel your body, your breath.
And when you are ready, gently open your eyes. Sat nam.

## Script — Seven Chakras
(Long — ~8–10 min. Read slowly with long pauses at each centre.)

Welcome to the seven chakra meditation. Sit with your spine tall, and gently close your eyes.
Take a deep breath in, through the nose. And breathe out. Again, breathe in. And release. (… pause …)
We will travel through the seven centres of energy, from the base of the spine to the crown.

Bring your attention to the base of your spine. This is Muladhara, the root chakra. See a deep red light,
glowing softly like an ember. Breathe into this space. Silently repeat: I am safe. I am grounded. (long pause)

Rise to just below the navel. This is Svadhisthana, the sacral chakra. See a warm orange light, flowing
like water. Breathe into this space. Silently repeat: I feel. I create. (long pause)

Rise to the solar plexus, above the navel. This is Manipura. See a bright yellow light, like the morning
sun. Breathe into this space. Silently repeat: I am strong. I am confident. (long pause)

Rise to the centre of your chest. This is Anahata, the heart chakra. See an emerald green light, soft and
open. Breathe into your heart. Silently repeat: I love. And I am loved. (long pause)

Rise to the throat. This is Vishuddha. See a clear blue light, like the open sky. Breathe into this space.
Silently repeat: I speak my truth, with kindness. (long pause)

Rise to the space between your eyebrows. This is Ajna, the third eye. See a deep indigo light, still and
wise. Breathe into this space. Silently repeat: I see clearly. I trust my inner wisdom. (long pause)

Rise to the crown of your head. This is Sahasrara, the thousand-petal lotus. See a violet-white light,
opening like a flower. Breathe into this space. Silently repeat: I am connected, to all that is. (long pause)
Let the light pour through you, from crown to root. (long pause)

All seven centres are awake and aligned. The body is relaxed. The mind is calm. The spirit is positive.
Rest in this column of light. (long pause) Slowly bring your awareness back to the room. Feel your fingers
and your toes. And when you are ready, gently open your eyes. Namaste.
